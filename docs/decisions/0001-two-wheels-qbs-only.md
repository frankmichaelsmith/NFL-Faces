# 0001 — Two wheels, quarterbacks only, single seasons from 2000

**Date:** 2026-09-09 · **Decided by:** Frank

The spec's three wheels (era, team, position) become two (season, team). Only quarterbacks are in the game. Seasons are single years starting in 2000.

**Why.** Quarterbacks are the only NFL position whose faces casual fans recognize; RB/WR/TE rolls would have been homework. Single seasons remove the era table and make every combo have exactly one answer. Starting at 2000 keeps the missing-photo list at 44 people rather than thousands.

**Numbers behind it (ESPN pull, 2026-09-09):** 830 combos, 192 unique QBs, 44 without an ESPN headshot, all from 2000–2009. 2010→ is 100% covered.

**Consequence.** The engine keeps wheel count and role list as config so positions or another league can return later.
