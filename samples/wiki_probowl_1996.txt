The '''1997 [[Pro Bowl]]''' was the [[National Football League|NFL]]'s [[all-star game]] for the [[1996 NFL season|1996 season]]. The game was played on February 2, 1997, at [[Aloha Stadium]] in [[Honolulu, Hawaii]]. The final score was [[American Football Conference|AFC]] 26, [[National Football Conference|NFC]] 23.<ref>{{cite news|title=AFC finally gets its kicks|url=https://www.tampabay.com/archive/1997/02/03/afc-finally-gets-its-kicks/|work=[[Tampa Bay Times]]|date=February 3, 1997|access-date=February 20, 2024}}</ref> [[Mark Brunell]] of the [[Jacksonville Jaguars]] was the game's [[Most valuable player|MVP]].  In the game, Brunell threw for 236 yards.  He connected with the [[Oakland Raiders]] [[Tim Brown (American football)|Tim Brown]] for an 80-yard touchdown to tie the game at 23 with only 44 seconds to go.

The referee was [[Larry Nemmers]].

To date, this is the most recent Pro Bowl that went to overtime.
